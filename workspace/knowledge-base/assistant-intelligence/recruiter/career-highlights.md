# Career Highlights & STAR Stories

This document provides concrete behavioral evidence for recruiters and hiring managers.

## 1. Leadership & Operations (Transworld Home)
- **Situation:** The Technical Assistance Center was struggling with SLA enforcement across 50k users.
- **Task:** Needed to manage a 14-person team to drastically reduce resolution times.
- **Action:** Restructured workflows, implemented strict SLA tracking, and mentored L1/L2 engineers.
- **Result:** Reduced ISP faults and resolution times by 18%.

## 2. Technical Scaling (NovaSole Pakistan)
- **Situation:** The e-commerce platform needed reliable inventory and payment sync across three sales channels at 500k+ monthly visitors.
- **Task:** Eliminate a manual daily reconciliation process without breaking checkout.
- **Action:** Built automated workflows using REST APIs and webhook-based triggers, plus data pipelines for multi-channel synchronisation.
- **Result:** 98%+ data accuracy and removal of the previously manual daily reconciliation process.

## 3. Production RAG and voice agents
- **Situation:** Operations needed an AI agent connected to real systems of record, not a standalone chatbot.
- **Task:** Deploy an LLM-powered agent across WhatsApp and inbound voice.
- **Action:** Built a LangChain RAG pipeline using OpenAI, Gemini, and Groq-hosted Llama 3.3 70B; connected the agent via a 16-node n8n graph and an MCP server; added fallback handling and Presidio PII redaction before data reached the LLM.
- **Result:** 1,000+ daily production interactions and the High Performance Excellence Award (June 2025). Role concluded July 2026.

## 4. Applied AI (Cygnus Technologies)
- **Situation:** The business needed end-to-end AI/ML systems for large-scale data processing and decision making.
- **Task:** Design and deploy production AI covering ETL, RAG, analytics, and MLOps.
- **Action:** Build scalable ETL pipelines, custom ML/LLM/RAG applications with LangChain, LlamaIndex, Hugging Face, and OpenAI APIs, AWS rollout, Presidio redaction, and hallucination evals.
- **Result:** Current role as Senior Applied AI/ML Engineer (July 2026 – Present).

## 5. Applied AI instructorship (Bano Qabil / Alkhidmat)
- **Situation:** Beginners needed a path into applied AI that mapped to production work, not theory-only notebooks.
- **Task:** Design and deliver a hands-on curriculum as a part-time volunteer trainer.
- **Action:** Teach Python, LLMs, prompt engineering, RAG, agents, embeddings, vector databases, and MLOps fundamentals through project work.
- **Result:** Concurrent Trainer, Applied Artificial Intelligence role (August 2026 – Present).
